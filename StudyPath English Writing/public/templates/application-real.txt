应用文真题套用模板

1，(2005) Directions: Two months ago you got a job as an editor for the magazine Designs & Fashions. But now you find that the work is not what you expected. You decide to quit. Write a letter to your boss, Mr. Wang, telling him your decision, stating your reason(s), and making an apology.
Write your letter with no less than 100 words.Write it neatly on ANSWER SHEET 2.Do not sign your own name at the end of the letter; use "Li Ming" instead.You do not need to write the address. (10 points)

Dear Mr. Wang,

I hope this letter finds you well. My purpose of this letter is to inform you of my decision to resign from my position as an editor.

During these two months, I have had a fulfilling and meaningful time. Your support, along with that of my coworkers, has broadened my horizons and enriched my knowledge. However, I am resigning because the salary, career goals, and working hours of this job don't match what I expected.

I deeply regret any inconvenience caused and hope that you can accept my resignation and understand my situation.

Yours sincerely,
Li Ming

2，(2006) Directions: You want to contribute to Project Hope by offering financial aid to a child in a remote area. Write a letter to the department concerned, asking them to help find a candidate. You should specify what kind of child you want to help and how you will carry out your plan.
Write your letter in no less than 100 words.
Write it neatly on ANSWER SHEET 2.
Do not sign your own name at the end of the letter; use "Li Ming" instead.
Do not write the address. (10 points)

Dear Sir or Madam,

I hope this letter finds you well. I, a senior student, am writing this letter to ask for your assistance in finding a candidate for my contribution to Project Hope.

First and foremost, I need your help to find a candidate, preferably a girl from the remote area. Furthermore, diligence, punctuality, and an approachable personality are the most desirable qualities I am looking for. What is more, with my expertise, I hope to offer inspirational advice to her on study and life, while also sponsoring her with 500 yuan per month. Last but not least, I will replace the old heating system with a brand-new one to provide her with a more comfortable environment for learning.

Looking forward to hearing from you at your earliest convenience.

Yours sincerely,
Li Ming

3，(2007) Directions: Write a letter to your university library, making suggestions for improving its service.
You should write about 100 words on ANSWER SHEET 2.
Do not sign your own name at the end of the letter. Use "Li Ming" instead.
Do not write the address. (10 points)

Dear Sir or Madam,

I hope this letter finds you well. I, a regular senior, am writing this letter to offer some suggestions for improving the library service.

First and foremost, I personally find it attractive to develop an online library app which enjoys widespread popularity among university students. It will definitely bring them a brand-new experience. What is more, the library should acquire more classics, because these books are very inspiring, and each reading of these books brings different insights. It's highly suitable for university students. Last but not least, the library should recruit volunteers to guide students to follow the relevant regulations, keep silence, and avoid littering.

Hopefully, the suggestions provided will be helpful to you.

Yours sincerely,
Li Ming

4，(2008) Directions: You have just come back from Canada and found a music CD in your luggage that you forgot to return to Bob, your landlord there. Write him a letter to
1) make an apology, and
2) suggest a solution.
You should write about 100 words on ANSWER SHEET 2.
Do not sign your own name at the end of the letter. Use "Li Ming" instead.
Do not write the address. (10 points)

Dear Bob,

I hope this letter finds you well. My purpose of this letter is to apologize for forgetting to return the music CD to you when I left Canada.

Life can be unpredictable and full of surprises. Yesterday, I received a sudden notification that I must attend a mandatory academic meeting, which forced me to change my plans and return to China today. When I opened the suitcase, I was shocked to find your CD in my bag. I feel truly sorry about this. As a remedy, I'd like to mail the CD back to you at my own expense, and I'll also send you a copy of Dream of the Red Chamber. This book is very inspiring, and each reading of this book brings different insights. It's highly suitable for foreigners like you.

Once again, I deeply regret any inconvenience caused and hope that you can accept my apology and understand my situation.

Yours sincerely,
Li Ming

5，(2009) Directions: Restrictions on the use of plastic bags have not been so successful in some regions. "White Pollution" is still going on.
Write a letter to the editor(s) of your local newspaper to
1) give your opinions briefly, and
2) make two or three suggestions.
You should write about 100 words on ANSWER SHEET 2.
Do not sign your own name at the end of the letter. Use "Li Ming" instead.
Do not write the address. (10 points)

Dear Editor,

I hope this letter finds you well. I, a senior student, am writing this letter to express my concern about "White Pollution" and make two suggestions to solve this problem.

First and foremost, we need a package deal, including issuing related laws and enhancing supervision. What is more, we also need to strengthen environmental education, encourage students to cherish the environment, and strengthen self-discipline. With the support of these measures, we can ultimately tackle the issue.

Thank you for your kind consideration and time. Hopefully, the suggestions provided will be helpful to you.

Yours sincerely,
Li Ming

6，(2010 英语一) Directions: You are supposed to write for the Postgraduates' Association a notice to recruit volunteers for an international conference on globalization. The notice should include the basic qualifications for applicants and other information which you think is relevant.
You should write about 100 words on ANSWER SHEET 2.
Do not sign your own name at the end of the notice. Use "Postgraduates' Association" instead. (10 points)

Notice

December 10, 2010

I hope this notice finds you well. An international conference on globalization will be held. The intention of this notice is to recruit volunteers.

First and foremost, the conference is scheduled to be held on December 26, 2010, at 2 PM in the Students' Recreation Center of our university, and it will last for 3 days. What is more, the event will consist of a series of activities, such as keynote speeches and group discussions. Our university president will deliver a welcome address to all participants. Last but not least, we need 10 volunteers, preferably those with relevant experience. Furthermore, a cooperative spirit, punctuality, and an approachable personality are the most desirable qualities we are looking for.

If you are interested in this activity, please scan the QR code to register.

Postgraduates' Association

7，(2010 英语二) Directions: You have just come back from the U.S. as a member of a Sino-American cultural exchange program. Write a letter to your American colleague to
1) express your thanks for his/her warm reception;
2) welcome him/her to visit China in due course.
You should write about 100 words on ANSWER SHEET 2.
Do not sign your own name at the end of the letter. Use "Zhang Wei" instead.
Do not write your address. (10 points)

Dear Bob,

I hope this letter finds you well. My purpose of this letter is to express my sincere thanks for your warm reception during the Sino-American cultural exchange program.

During my time in the United States, I have had a fulfilling and meaningful experience. Your support, along with that of my coworkers, has broadened my horizons and enriched my knowledge. A cooperative spirit, punctuality, and an approachable personality are the most desirable qualities that you possess, which made me feel extremely relaxed and happy when getting along with you. I personally find it attractive to invite you to visit China. I will take you to visit the Great Wall, because it embodies China's profound and diverse ancient culture and provides a fascinating glimpse into the country's rich history.

Looking forward to meeting you at your earliest convenience.

Yours sincerely,
Zhang Wei
